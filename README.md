The Rhythm of Temperature

About the Project

The project involves recording the temperatures that I experience each time I go outside over a span of 20 days and uses Processing (Java) to create a creative, continuous wave that goes from left to right.

Research Question

What happens to the temperature every time I go for a walk outside over a period of 20 days?

Data Collection

Every time I went out I noted down the temperature with the aim of gathering at least 100 observations. Each observation corresponds to the temperature at the time when I went outside. There are 5 observations per day.

The data sets the date, time, temperature, the temperature that we feel and the weather condition outside which were recorded during each observation.

SCAMPER Design Process

S — Substitute

I decided to use two continuous waves (one for the temperature that we feel and one for the actual temperature), rather than a traditional graph in order to present my temperature observations in a more creative manner.

C — Combine

To make the data more visually interesting, I combined the wave with colours, shapes, points, temperature labels, images and sound.

A — Adapt

I shaped the design to resemble two flowing waves in order to show how the temperature changes every time I go outside.

M — Modify

I changed the colours and appearance of the waves as well as the shapes to show the differences caused by colder and warmer temperatures.

P — Put to Another Use

I created a visual record of the temperatures I came across by drawing on the everyday experience of going outside.

E — Eliminate

I got rid of the unnecessary visual elements such as the time and location of when and where it happened so that attention could remain on the temperature observations and the wave.

R — Reverse / Rearrange

I ordered my observations from left to right according to time so that the viewers will be able to see the temperature changes over the 20 days.
